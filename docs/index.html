Abstract

TrajPrompt aligns AIS trajectories with natural-language text in a shared embedding space (a CLIP-style contrastive encoder), so a plain-English query retrieves matching trajectories. Candidates are confirmed with the author's TGARD group-rendezvous detector and, in the production path, a SAM 2 look at the Sentinel-2 chip.

Honest scope. Real and tested: the trajectory-CLIP encoder with its InfoNCE contrastive loss, and the TGARD group-rendezvous algorithm with pairwise haversine. Stubbed: the SAM 2 / Sentinel-2 chip puller (returns zeros). Scaffold: the trained traj-CLIP checkpoint and the AIS-text pairs dataset.
